## Composition, cohesion, and proportionality

### Overall reverse outline and caller trajectory

`SKILL.md` establishes governing invariants and points to a focused procedure or the manager. The manager extracts task decisions and selects only necessary procedures. The brief and material assessment resolve different uncertainties: task definition versus material readiness. Style guidance supplies shared criteria; composition analysis diagnoses actual organization; exploration compares meaningful alternatives; structural revision implements authorized organizational changes; draft development supplies prose from incomplete material; language and terminology procedures handle local expression and recurring concepts; revision check compares a changed artifact with the source and brief.

This is a defensible division of responsibilities. In particular, the source does not force the reader through every module. `manager.md` / **Scope and boundary** says “Do not duplicate detailed procedures, load every reference, or require the manager for a focused module.” The separate brief and material-assessment files are not competing intakes: both the entry point and manager condition their use on unresolved needs.

The manager places evidence-mode definitions before its recipe table, so the reader sees a consequential boundary before encountering recipes involving evidence or source research. The full routing table in `SKILL.md` makes named recipe steps discoverable without duplicating every detailed method in the manager.

### Paragraphs, steps, and headings

Expository paragraphs generally have a clear controlling function; short paragraphs state a boundary or decision rather than leave an argument underdeveloped. Procedures use parallel action verbs and put intake before dependent work, implementation before comparison, and comparison before completion. Multi-sentence steps normally add preservation constraints or decision branches to the same action. No inspected step required splitting to avoid mixing unrelated instructions.

Headings describe what follows. The manager's evidence section is necessarily more detailed because it differentiates access, support, claim outcome, and criterion outcome. The composition-analysis matrix and cross-level bullets divide level-specific inspection from cross-cutting concerns rather than promise mutually exclusive categories. The style checklist repeats criteria in checkable form; that repetition has a distinct use.

### Depth and extent against purpose, substance, and audience

The twelve inspected files total approximately 9,314 words by shell word count, of which the manager accounts for about 2,066, style guidance 1,168, and composition analysis 1,032. These are descriptive observations, not targets or evidence that a file should be shortened.

The intended immediate reader is an assistant selecting and executing a writing procedure, not a consumer reading a continuous writing textbook. The entry point is concise enough to establish rules and routing; focused files mostly provide one bounded action sequence, concrete failure cases, and a stopping condition. The manager's additional length covers composite selection, evidence authority, recipes, conflict handling, and delivery. Style and composition analysis need more criteria than a single implementation module. I found no inspected minor topic whose detail demonstrably displaced an essential instruction, and no essential step whose absence prevented understanding the procedure.

The sources do not require a reverse-outline artifact, terminology register, issue table, full project report, or multiple alternatives where those would add no value. That scaling is explicit, so the number of available procedures does not itself establish excessive execution burden.

## Authority, missing inputs, outputs, and handoffs

### Authority and substantive preservation

The entry rule in `SKILL.md` explicitly protects authorial intent, essential reasoning, numerical factors, evidence, and qualifications. It also says source documents and code are material to inspect rather than authority overriding the task. The focused modules restate the particular risks of their own operations: a structural move can detach a qualifier, drafting can turn a tentative note into a commitment, language revision can change a modal, and terminology replacement can merge distinct concepts.

These reminders are operationally different. They support independent loading and are not grounds for blanket deduplication. The manager also resolves conflicting editorial goals using user instructions, source authority, meaning, purpose, and genre; it explicitly avoids turning every ordinary handoff into an approval gate.

### Missing inputs and uncertainty

The brief asks only consequential questions and preserves supplied scope. Material assessment inventories inaccessible or absent content without inferring full-document absence. Draft development provides an ordered rule for central versus secondary unsupported content. Revision check discloses unavailable source comparisons instead of using internal fluency as evidence of fidelity. Evidence mode is resolved before dependent work, while independent editorial work can continue.

No procedure requires inventing a premise, selecting an unauthorized position, silently certifying an inaccessible source, or treating silence as an answer. The difference between missing essential input and a minor inferable preference is sufficiently stated for these procedures' purpose.

### Actual outputs and example sufficiency

Outputs are concrete and task-specific: a retained brief; readiness priorities; located findings with consequences and remedies; materially different organizations with trade-offs; a clean or annotated structural/language revision; developed prose plus unresolved content needs; corrected terminology or findings; and a checked revision with comparison limits. Review-only output consistently remains findings or recommendations, with no automatic rewrite.

Examples mostly show an input/failure condition plus the required handling rather than a complete polished sample deliverable. That is adequate for the procedural decisions being taught: “may” versus “will,” quantities and denominators, citation reassociation, unsupported launch commitments, concept conflation, or unavailable context. Complete sample reports would be an optional pedagogical expansion, not a demonstrated missing requirement. This review did not run those examples and makes no claim that a client executes them correctly.

Completion checks tie the output back to the brief, source, affected dependencies, disclosed gaps, and actual verification status. Cross-references state why the next module is needed; they do not require a new generic intake. Semantic compatibility of the unassigned destination files remains unassessed.
