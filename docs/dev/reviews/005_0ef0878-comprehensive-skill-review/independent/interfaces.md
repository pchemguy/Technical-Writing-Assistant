### Authority and handoffs

The entry says "Review-only tasks produce findings" (`SKILL.md:17`). Argument review returns critique rather than advocacy (24); outline review recommends without automatic rewriting (15, 62); genre outputs follow the requested review or revision. Manager 76 limits action to authorized repairs and manager 114 states, "Do not make every handoff an approval gate." Revision check 13 explicitly skips review-only work without a revised artifact; its repair step 25 concerns ordinary introduced defects within existing authority. This preserves useful critique, conditional repairs, author decisions, and routine editorial progress without granting review-only tasks repair authority.

### Exactly two modes and acquisition boundary

Manager 36–46 defines external research/verification and review without external research; 48 says source inspection is "not a third mode." Selection 61–65 applies only when relevant and unresolved, retains existing decisions, and does not treat silence as selection. The entry 18 and 24 and evidence-review 9 provide the same gate for direct invocation.

The non-research rule is operational: "Acquire explicitly identified task targets through permitted access" (`manager.md:44`) but "Do not retrieve citation-only support, follow bibliographies, or search for corroboration." Evidence-review 11 repeats the distinction, restrictions, consequential URL ambiguity, and inaccessible-target handling; source-research 9 assigns target intake outside its research workflow. Thus inspecting a specifically requested linked README can proceed in non-research mode, while independently retrieving its supporting citations cannot. Explicit access restrictions still control both.
