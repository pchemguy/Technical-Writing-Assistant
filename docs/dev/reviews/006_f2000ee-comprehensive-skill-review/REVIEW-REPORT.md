### C-004: Evidence

- **Two evidence modes and target acquisition:** `SKILL.md` (line 18), `manager.md` (lines 36–65), `evidence-review.md` (lines 9–11), and `source-research.md` (lines 5, 9) consistently define exactly two modes (`External research and verification` and `Review without external research`). Bounded acquisition of explicitly identified task targets (such as repository files or requested appendices) is permitted in non-research mode for inspection, whereas independent retrieval of citation-only URLs, bibliography following, and corroborating search are prohibited in non-research mode, and explicit `no-network`, `no-retrieval`, or `supplied-content-only` restrictions override even target acquisition.
- **Separation of reasoning, support, and retrieval:** `argument-review.md` critiques internal reasoning and inference standards (deductive validity vs. probabilistic calibration, correlation vs. causation, unit/denominator compatibility) and hands empirical premise verification to `evidence-review.md`; `evidence-review.md` evaluates claim-to-support relationships across both modes and hands permitted external retrieval to `source-research.md`.
- **Outcome taxonomy:** `manager.md` (lines 50–57), `evidence-review.md` (lines 19, 26), `source-research.md` (line 20), and `outline-review.md` (lines 51, 54) keep four dimensions distinct:
    1. **Provenance/access:** `inspected`, `supplied but uninspected`, `inferred`, `recalled`, or `proposed`.
    2. **Source-to-claim relationship:** `supports`, `partially supports`, `contradicts`, or `does not address` (with `not assessed` for unavailable content).
    3. **Researched-claim outcome:** `supported`, `partially supported`, `contradicted`, or `unresolved` within the scoped inspected evidence.
    4. **Criterion outcome:** `satisfied`, `partly satisfied` (for identifiable subparts), `failed`, or `not assessable` (plus `not applicable` only when genuinely outside scope).

### C-005: Outlines and genres

- **Contextual outlines and maps:** `outline-context-exploration.md`, `outline-generation.md`, and `outline-review.md` adapt hierarchies, tables, relationship maps, and combined views to the task context without a fixed profile catalogue or mandatory schema. `outline-review.md` (lines 23–27) provides selectable `Evaluative only`, `Validation only`, and `Comprehensive` coverage sharing one intake inventory and consolidating overlapping findings (lines 31, 60, 67). It explicitly distinguishes topic presence from explanatory adequacy and from explicit content-note requirements (line 66), and distinguishes valid non-prerequisite cycles (such as two-way operational communication) from defective prerequisite cycles (lines 47, 72).
- **Genre boundaries:**
    - `software-documentation.md` covers READMEs, tutorials/procedures, API references, architecture documents, and docstrings; separates static inspection from authorized command execution (lines 16, 40–42); and preserves declared or consistently established project docstring conventions with Google style as the Python default only when no governing convention exists (line 25, matching `SPEC.md` line 18).
    - `scientific-writing.md` separates observations from interpretation, preserves negative/mixed results and sample denominators, and prohibits inventing methods, calibration details, statistical significance, effect sizes, or p-values.
    - `professional-writing.md` separates facts, analysis, recommendations, requests, and commitments across reports, proposals, decision briefs, correspondence, and executive summaries, prohibiting invented approvals, promises, or commercial terms and never treating drafting as authorization to send.
