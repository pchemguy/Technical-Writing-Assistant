## Style, composition and size/detail judgment

The package's organization matches its reader: an agent selecting an operational procedure. The entry establishes immediate constraints before routing. Focused references normally progress from scope and inputs to actions, outputs, examples and completion. This pattern enables independent loading; it is not fragmentation into arbitrary headings. The manager's contents and recipe table support navigation through the larger coordination responsibility. Outline review's contents help distinguish its two substantial activities.

Paragraphs and numbered steps mostly have one controlling concern. Conditions sit near the action they constrain: missing content before assertive drafting, evidence authority before retrieval, protected material before editing, and comparison limits before fidelity claims. Examples test concrete distinctions rather than merely restating goals. Terms such as “working contract” are defined locally; triage, diagnosis, exploration, revision and checking are not used interchangeably.

These are reproducible size observations, not token estimates, ceilings or cost measurements. Most remaining focused files are approximately 460–790 words. Their procedures and boundaries supply substance appropriate to the breadth claimed.

No general shortening, merge or split is supported by this review. The longer files contain distinct decisions, evaluative criteria and examples needed for their responsibility; smaller references have meaningful independent triggers. Entry routing and manager recipes overlap in topic but answer different questions: which module to load versus how to coordinate a composite task. The capability map's repeated style policy preserves governing design outside the runtime package; it is not necessarily loaded by a consumer.

Meaning-preservation, source authority and missing-information safeguards recur because the relevant reference may be loaded independently. Their local copies protect different actions: intake, drafting, rearrangement, wording and final comparison. The manager remains the canonical evidence-mode owner; local evidence modules carry prerequisites and a link back. No divergent second full procedure was identified.
