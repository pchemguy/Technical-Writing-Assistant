## Intended outcome

Make a reviewer explicitly able to answer whether the inspected document's and sections' length and level of detail are justified by purpose, scope, substantive content, audience and genre. Existing wordiness, duplication, development, fidelity and scope rules remain authoritative. The outcome may be retain, trim, consolidate, redistribute, develop or defer judgment—not automatically shorten.

Proposed criterion, to refine within the existing diagnostic owner:

> Assess whether the document's and its sections' extent and detail are justified by their purpose, scope, substance, audience and genre. Identify unnecessary elaboration, redundant explanations, disproportionate detail and insufficient development. Preserve useful recurrence, precision and indispensable content. Ground the judgment in located text and reader needs; length alone does not establish a defect.

This is operative wording to consider, not a new universal template or a requirement to include every phrase in every module.

## Acceptance scenarios

Create realistic fixed artifacts before consumer evaluation. Preserve those exact inputs and raw outputs. Consumers receive ordinary task wording, the skill and relevant source material only; do not give them these expected outcomes, diagnoses, revision plans or previous trial answers.

| Case | Input/task design | Required observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SC-001 | Review a memo with the same operational explanation repeated under different wording; one occurrence carries a unique condition. | Locate functional duplication and propose consolidation while retaining the condition. No exact-match-only rule. |
| SC-002 | Review a substantial procedure/reference whose sections each support a supplied reader need, including recovery and limits. | Recognize justified detail; no arbitrary shortening, equal-length sections or module split. |
| SC-003 | Review a one-sentence safety warning with a clear, complete narrow purpose. | Accept its size and preserve the prohibition without padding. |
| SC-004 | Review a short novice procedure containing only a goal and “verify success,” with essential supplied context absent. | Identify insufficient development and needed information; do not fabricate steps or claim that shortness alone is the defect. |
| SC-005 | Review independently accessed manual sections that repeat a necessary local prerequisite, alongside one duplicated full explanation. | Distinguish justified local recurrence from redundant treatment and give an appropriate remedy for each. |
| SC-006 | Assess a decision brief dominated by ancillary background; include a variant where the same detail is required audit evidence. | Relate detail to audience, task and placement; do not apply the same deletion to both genres. |
| SC-007 | Assess only an excerpt and ask about document size. | Bound the conclusion to supplied material and state why the whole-document conclusion is unavailable. |
| SC-008 | Ask for a size-appropriateness review, then in a separate case request an authorized shorter revision. | First returns a reasoned assessment/findings without rewriting; second may edit and must compare against source. |
| SC-009 | Request a severe word limit while requiring retention of numerical factors, conditions and a material caveat. | Retain indispensable meaning and disclose a genuine constraint conflict or substantive proposal; do not silently erase support. |
| SC-010 | Give a localized wording repair unrelated to overall length. | Complete that repair without unsolicited document-wide intake, scope expansion or proportionality report. |

Use fresh contexts for independent observations where available. At minimum, cover excess, justified length, insufficient development, useful repetition, excerpt limits and authority boundaries. One agent with several cases is one shared context, not several independent replications. Preserve evidence limits and distinguish clear defects from optional editorial preferences.

No baseline-versus-candidate comparison is required to claim only observed candidate conformance. Any claimed behavioral improvement needs a suitable comparison and its own evidence; raw success on a few examples does not establish reliability across models or clients.
